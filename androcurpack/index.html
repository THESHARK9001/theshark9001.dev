DOWNLOAD androcurpack.7z

Requirements:

7-zip or WinRAR


Instructions for installing on Windows:

1. Using 7-zip or WinRAR, extract "androcurpack.7z"

2. Open the "androcurpack" folder

3. Right click on "Install.inf"

4. Click "Install" (if on Windows 11, click "Show more options" at the bottom, then "Install")

5. Press the Windows key and type "Pointer" and click "Change how the mouse pointer looks"

6. Under "Scheme" select "androcurpack"

7. Click "Apply"
